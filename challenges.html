How PhotoRun works

ISTEDA is bringing photo challenges to Dubai Run so you can meet other runners and have some fun along the route. Take part and see how high you can climb on the ISTEDA RUN ALONG challenge leaderboard.

  1. Choose one of the three levels and open a challenge.
  2. Complete the task, take a photo as proof, and upload it under that challenge.
  3. Try all three levels. Each completed challenge earns 1 point in Level 1, 3 points in Level 2, or 5 points in Level 3. Your points add up across levels, and the highest total takes the top spot.

Download before the run

Mobile data may be unreliable during Dubai Run. Save the challenge PDF before you start, take your photos offline, and upload them here right after the run when you’re back online.
