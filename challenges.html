Game Rules

ISTEDA Community is bringing a fun photo challenge to Dubai Run 2026. Complete as many challenges as you can and upload the requested photos. The highest cumulative score wins the EL FORSA PhotoRun Challenge, powered by ISTEDA Community.

There are three levels with five challenges each. Upload the requested photo for each challenge you complete. Up to three completed challenges per level count toward your score, for a maximum of 3, 6, and 9 points respectively.

  • Level 1 · 1 point per challenge
  • Level 2 · 2 points per challenge
  • Level 3 · 3 points per challenge

Special Awards

  • Best Photography · 4 points · Submit any photo related to Dubai Run.
  • Best Selfie · 4 points · Take a selfie with something related to Dubai.
  • Most Calories Burned · Upload a screenshot or other proof of your total calories burned on the day.

Reminder · Download before the run

Mobile data may be unreliable during Dubai Run. Download the PhotoRun PDF before you start, take your photos offline, and upload your proof right after the run when you’re back online.
