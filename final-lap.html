ISTEDA × EL FORSA

Final Lap

Community gathering

Sunday, 29 November 2026 · Kite Beach, Dubai

Meet at ADCB Metro in Karama at 12:30 PM. We’ll head to Kite Beach together for the Fitness Village and airshow.

The plan

  1. 12:30 PM

    Meet at ADCB Metro Station, Karama

    Take the Red Line to Mall of the Emirates, then Bus 81 towards Dubai Mall. Get off at Abdallah Mleih Mosque and walk to Kite Beach together.

  2. Around 2:00–4:15 PM

    Explore the Kite Beach Fitness Village

    Try an available activity, play beach football or join a swim if the lifeguard gives the go-ahead.

    View Fitness Village activities

    Entry is free with on-site registration.

    Nissan Navara Pro Obstacle Course
    Test your balance and coordination as you work through the obstacles.
    Skechers Beach Warrior
    Take on a fitness challenge on the sand.
    Amazon Rebounder
    Try a group workout on mini-trampolines.
    Etisalat Main Stage
    Drop by for whichever group workout is on when we arrive.
    More to explore
    Spin and circuit training, boxing and MMA, Pilates and the Emirates Skate Park.
  3. 4:15–5:15 PM

    Fursan Al Emarat Air Show

    Watch the aerial display together from Kite Beach.

  4. 5:15 PM

    Sunset and karak

    Grab some snacks and get everyone together for a crew photo.

  5. 6:30 PM

    Head back to Karama

    Walk to the Abu Manara Mosque – 01 bus stop. Take Bus 81 to Mall of the Emirates, then the metro back to ADCB.

What to bring

Bring your EL FORSA cap, a topped-up nol card, swimwear, a towel, a change of clothes and drinking water.

Bring cash for your own food and drinks.
